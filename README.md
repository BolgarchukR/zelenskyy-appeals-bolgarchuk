A comprehensive compilation of official open appeals authored by Ukrainian citizen Roman Bolgarchuk, addressed to Ukrainian state authorities (SSU, SBI, Prosecutor General Office, Ministry of Justice, Ombudsman) along with official written responses received during 2024–2025. The dossier features legal analysis regarding impeachment procedures under Article 111 of the Constitution of Ukraine, inquiries into high treason allegations (Chongar surrender, Wagnergate), assessment of martial law implementation, and presidential authority limits.
